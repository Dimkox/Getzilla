# Rollback and recovery

Stop release or activation on failing exact-head checks, stale reviews, ambiguous authority, installation conflicts, or admission bypass. Keep paid execution disabled.

Recover source through a reviewed forward fix or revert PR. Regenerate harness mirrors from canonical sources. Never bypass branch protection to restore source.

Do not downgrade the SQLite journal after schema 3 admission records exist. Preserve accepted jobs and all reservation records, especially dispatching or unknown spend. Source rollback cannot erase incurred cost or grant replay authority. Test recovery against a copied journal before an authorized runtime change.

Consumer files remain untouched in this task. Ownership manifests and quarantine plans do not authorize deletion. Any later authorized quarantine must retain exact hashes, modes, recovery paths and restore-to-absent checks. Unknown ownership stays preserved.

After a repair, repeat bounded controls, independent reviews and exact-head gates. Host activation needs its own allow/deny audit before acceptance. Provider revocation requires provider evidence; an empty environment does not prove revocation.
